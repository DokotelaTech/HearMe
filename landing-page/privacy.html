Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

At HearMe, your privacy and psychological safety are our absolute top priorities. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign up or log in using social authentication (like Google, Facebook, or Apple), we only request the absolute minimum information required to secure your account:

  • Your primary email address.
  • Your basic profile name (which remains private and is not displayed publicly on your anonymous profile).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account.
  • Provide account recovery options.
  • Send critical service updates or security alerts.

3. Data Protection & Anonymity

HearMe is designed as a safe space. Your public interactions on the platform are tied to your anonymous username. We intentionally separate your real identity (gathered during sign-up) from your public activity to ensure your healing journey remains private.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We will only share data if compelled by law enforcement to protect the physical safety of a user or the public.
